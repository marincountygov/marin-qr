Create a QR code

Link

Any HTTPS link. You can make a QR code for any secure website.

Which websites can I use?
  • marincounty.gov and sites under it, such as hhs.marincounty.gov
  • marinhhs.org and sites under it
  • marincountygov.github.io only
  • marinmap.org and sites under it
Tracking details (optional)

Tracking details can show where people found your link. They do not change the page. Ask your web team which names and values to use.
